What we do

Design & build

A complete, mobile-first website written in your voice — services, prices, doctor or team profiles, reviews, photos, directions. Fast to load, easy to read on any phone.

Online booking systems

Real appointments, not a contact form pretending. Patients pick a service, a day and a time slot inside your actual working hours — and get a proper confirmation.

Staff dashboards

Your team sees the day's bookings in one place, confirms or cancels, blocks leave days, and exports the list. No spreadsheets, no missed calls.

Local SEO basics

Page titles, descriptions and structured data written the way people actually search — “dermatologist in Medipally”, not jargon. Set up right from day one.

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Brief

    You tell us about the business — services, hours, prices, photos. One short document, no meetings that could have been an email.

  2. 2

    Build

    We design and build the complete site, booking included, and keep you posted with a link you can open on your phone.

  3. 3

    Tested end to end

    We book real test appointments, watch them land in the dashboard, fill a slot, block a day — and clean the test data out before handover.

  4. 4

    Handover pack

    You get the logins and a short plain-language guide, so your own team can run the site day to day without us.

The problem

Findable, but not bookable

Patients could find the clinic's name on listing sites — but some listings still carried an old address, and none of them could take an appointment. Every booking meant a phone call during clinic hours, or a walk-in and a wait. There was no single, correct place on the internet that said who the clinic is, what it treats, when it is open — and let a patient act on it.

  • Outdated listings: an old address still showing on listing platforms, sending patients the wrong way.
  • No way to book: no online appointment option at all — phone and walk-ins only.
  • No front desk system: the day's appointments lived in calls and memory, with nothing the staff could glance at, confirm, or export.

The product

What was delivered

A complete, working system — designed, built, tested and handed over as one product.

  • A 10-page website: home, the doctor, treatments with a page for each, a products showroom, reviews, booking, contact and directions, plus privacy, terms and a medical disclaimer — mobile-first, in the clinic's own colours, fast on any phone.
  • Real online booking: the patient picks a service, then a date (Monday to Saturday), then a 15-minute slot inside actual clinic hours — 11 AM–1 PM and 5–8 PM. Two patients per slot; when a slot fills, it closes on its own. Sundays and the clinic's leave days are closed automatically.
  • The clinic's own database: every booking is saved instantly to the clinic's cloud database. Nothing sits in an inbox waiting to be noticed.
  • A staff dashboard: the clinic logs in to see the day's appointment list, mark visit statuses, block leave and holiday dates, read website enquiries, and export the day's list as a CSV. The dashboard is staff-login only — patients never see it.
  • One-tap WhatsApp confirmation: the patient confirms the booking on WhatsApp to the clinic's number, +91 93926 31157, and call, WhatsApp, booking and directions stay one thumb-tap away on every page.
  • Honest content only: the clinic's real reviews, attributed; treatment pages that explain what to expect without promising results; result photos only with written patient consent. No stock claims, no invented numbers.

See it live

The staff dashboard is login-only and is not linked publicly. The clinic's team received its login and a plain-language handover guide at delivery.

Proof of testing

Tested like a patient would use it

Before handover, the whole journey was run end to end in a real browser, against the live system — not a staging copy.

  • A test booking was placed through the public website and watched as it arrived in the clinic's dashboard, with the right service, date and slot.
  • Full slots were verified: once a slot reached its two-patient capacity, the website stopped offering it.
  • Closed days were verified: Sundays offer no slots, and a day blocked in the dashboard disappears from booking immediately.
  • Clean at handover: every test booking and test record was removed before delivery, so the clinic started with an empty, working system.
